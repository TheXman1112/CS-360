# CS-360

## 1. Briefly summarize the requirements and goals of the app you developed. What user needs was this app designed to address?

My app is an inventory tracking application that allows authorized users to log in and manage warehouse inventory. Users can add, remove, and adjust item quantities, view inventory in a grid, and enable notifications for low stock.

## 2. What screens and features were necessary to support user needs and produce a user-centered UI for the app? How did your UI designs keep users in mind? Why were your designs successful?

The app includes a login screen, inventory grid, settings and notifications screen, and an add-item workflow. I used consistent green and blue colors and simple navigation to create an organized, user-friendly interface that follows Android development guidelines.

## 3. How did you approach the process of coding your app? What techniques or strategies did you use? How could those be applied in the future?

I initially tried adding too many features at once, which made development difficult. I changed my approach by building a working application first and adding features individually. In future projects, I will focus on core functionality before expanding the app.

## 4. How did you test to ensure your code was functional? Why is this process important and what did it reveal?

I tested the app using Android Studio emulators with different screen sizes, including phones and tablets. This helped me understand the importance of ensuring an application works properly across different devices and provides a consistent user experience.

## 5. Considering the full app design and development process, from initial planning to finalization, where did you have to innovate to overcome a challenge?

I struggled to connect the notification screen to the settings menu and configure the radio button. I used online tutorials to learn how to implement the feature. This taught me the importance of researching unfamiliar concepts and applying what I learn to solve problems.

## 6. In what specific component from your mobile app were you particularly successful in demonstrating your knowledge, skills, and experience?

I enjoyed developing the inventory grid. I originally struggled getting it to appear, but I was able to build a lot of the functionality behind it with no issues. I really enjoyed the challenge of getting it all to work.
